## About

**Ready or Not We Go** is a narrative-driven time management game by Slafurry Studios. You play as the founder of a game development studio, managing a team of specialists through intense sprint deadlines.

### The Premise

It's crunch time. Your team has 24 hours to complete all tasks before the sprint deadline. Every decision matters — who works on what, when they rest, and whether you can gather enough evidence to call in reinforcements before it's too late.

But be careful: your team members are human. They get tired. They fail. And when they fail at the deadline... they're gone.

## Gameplay

### Time Management

- **Real-time clock**: Time ticks down in real-time. Every second counts.
- **2-hour sprint**: At the start, tasks are locked during an initial sprint period. Use this time to plan.
- **24-hour deadline**: All tasks must be completed before time runs out, or your team faces termination.

### Team Management

- **Multiple team members**, each with different roles (Programmer, Composer, Artist, Designer, Founder)
- **Energy system**: Working drains energy. Sleep restores it — but takes game time.
- **Team members sleep independently**: They'll rest when their tasks are done, not when you do.
- **Permanent termination**: Miss the deadline with incomplete tasks, and that team member is gone forever — taking their unfinished work with them.

### The Founder

You play as the Founder with unique advantages:
- **Unlimited energy**: Work without tiring
- **2× execution speed**: Complete actions faster than other team members
- **Task inheritance**: Take over unfinished tasks from terminated team members

### Memory System

Collect memories and information as you work:
- Each action can grant memories/information
- Your memory bank tracks everything you've uncovered
- The more evidence you gather, the better your outcome

### Call Authorities

When you're ready, call the authorities with your collected evidence:

- **Less than 10 proof**: Worst ending — they won't help
- **10+ proof**: Bad ending — minimal response
- **20+ proof**: Mediocre ending — some investigation
- **35+ proof**: Good ending — serious investigation
- **50+ proof**: Best ending — authorities take full action

## Controls

- **Mouse/Keyboard**: Navigate UI, select tasks, click targets in the pendulum mini-game
- **Double-tap**: Force wake from deep sleep during the mini-game
